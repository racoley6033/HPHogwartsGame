# Hogwarts Adventure Game 

## Specifications
The chants adventure game will start by showing you where you are, where you can go and the items and enemies in your current location. From this, you can pick up or drop those items, fight those enemies or move to a different area. Picking up items will give you the ability to add onto your health, which is your combat ability. If your health number/combat ability is higher than the enemies health or combat ability, you will defeat them in battle. You can repeat this for every area you go and fight different types of enemies. The items and enemies location are randomized. You can keep going until you choose to exit.

## Objective
The objective of our game is to get rid of every enemy on the map

## vocabulary
Our story is based off of the movie, Harry Potter, so all related enemies and places and items will be based off of this movie for further information please access the Harry Potter fan website.

## player
our default player is Harry Potter. This cannot be changed. As a player, you have the capability to pick up items, drop items, move to different zones and fight monsters. These items add onto your health to give you the capability of winning. Your default health is 100.

## assets
All current items//assets are meant to add onto your health or combat ability. These items are randomized drop in different areas around our map.

## monsters
Monsters are enemies that randomly drop around the area with default health. If your player Harry Potter has more health or compatibility than you will win, if you have less health than the monster, you will lose. That is the basics of combat ability.
