AdventureGameMap.hpp

Represents the adventure game map. The AdventureGameMap class manages the locations (nodes) in the game, allowing for adding nodes, printing the map, and retrieving nodes by their ID.

Private Members:

  • locations: A vector of nodes representing locations in the game.

Methods:

  • buildMapNodes: Builds and connects map nodes using themes from Harry Potter.
  • PrintMap: Prints the names of all nodes in the map.
  • AddNode: Adds a new node to the map.
  • GetNode: Retrieves a node by its ID.

Player.hpp

The Player class represents a player in the game, with attributes such as name, health, strength, and inventory of assets/items.

Private Members:

  • name: The name of the player.
  • health: The health of the player, set to 100 by default.
  • strength: The strength of the player.
  • inventory: A vector of assets/items in the player's inventory.

Methods:

  • GetName: Gets the name of the player.
  • GetHealth: Gets the health of the player.
  • SetHealth: Sets the health of the player.
  • GetStrength: Gets the strength of the player.
  • AddAsset: Adds an asset to the player's inventory.
  • RemoveAsset: Removes an asset from the player's inventory by name.
  • DisplayInventory: Displays the player's inventory.
  • Fight: Gets the combat effectiveness of the player based on strength.
  • ChangeHealth: Changes the player's health by a specified amount (positive or negative).

Combatant.hpp

The Combatant class provides the basic attributes and methods for any character that can engage in combat, including players and monsters.

Protected Members:

  • _name: The name of the combatant.
  • _health: The health of the combatant.
  • _fightCoefficient: The combat effectiveness coefficient of the combatant.

Methods:

  • Fight: Gets the combat effectiveness coefficient.
  • GetName: Gets the name of the combatant.
  • GetHealth: Gets the current health of the combatant.
  • SetHealth: Sets the health of the combatant.
  • IsDefeated: Checks if the combatant is defeated (health is 0 or less).
  • CalculateDamage: Calculates the damage dealt by an attacker to a defender based on their fight coefficients.
  • ExecuteCombat: Simulates a combat sequence between two combatants until one is defeated.
